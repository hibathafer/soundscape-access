PRD · Product RequirementsSoundscape Access

Status: Approved
Scope said “open the app, keep it in front of you — flash + icons.” Now we know exactly: one screen with two buttons, a gentle permission step, a “Working now” toast, five fixed categories, swipeable messenger-style bubbles, red/orange/white color rules, unknown-sound handling, and the fallback if leaving the app proves technically impossible.

The Core Journey

Journey: Open app (one screen, two buttons) → first use: gentle mic explanation → system permission → Start → toast “Working now” → user leaves the app (session continues, phone usable) → live loop: sound arrives → flash from source side + icon bubble → user glances/acts → source recedes → flash fades → repeat (unknown → white “?” · two sounds → two bubbles · optional vibration) → Stop ends the session.
  1. Open app — one screen: large Start/Stop button in the center, small vibration toggle.
  2. First use — gentle one-line “why we need the microphone,” then the system permission dialog. Denied → friendly message + route to settings.
  3. Start — toast “Working now.”
  4. Leave the app — home screen or other apps; capture continues without obstructing phone use (fallback: session runs with the app in front if the platform refuses).
  5. Live loop — flash starts at the sound’s side and fades toward the opposite side; strength = sound strength; color = proximity (red near, orange farther, white unknown); swipeable icon bubble per sound; two sounds → two bubbles; optional vibration.
  6. Stop — press the center button again; the session ends.

Screens, Layout & Look and Feel

  • One screen only: center = large Start/Stop; secondary = small vibration toggle; live area = directional flash + floating bubbles.
  • Dark background (light is the hero) · warm & friendly (“an app that supports you, not one that shouts at you”) · fairly large, clear type.
  • Color rules: red = very close · orange = farther · white = type unknown. Color is never the only signal — position and strength always carry it too.

Features & Behavior — with acceptance criteria

Sound capture & classification — five fixed categories

Cars/traffic · speech/voices · door/knocking · alarm/bell/beep · birds/nature. These five are the only types shown in this version.

  • Car-horn sample played from one side → car icon bubble during the sound.
  • No other sound type is ever promised to the user.
Directional flash

Starts on the sound’s side, strongest toward the source, fading toward the opposite side; strength follows sound strength; shifts and disappears as the source/user moves away.

  • Sound from the right → flash starts right, dims toward the left.
  • Source stops/moves away → flash visibly fades out.
Sound-type bubbles (messenger chat-head style)

Floating, calm, non-blocking; category icon near the source direction; swipe down to dismiss.

  • Recognized sound shows its icon bubble; swiping removes it without ending the session.
Simultaneous sounds · Unknown sounds · Vibration
  • Two sounds from different sides → two bubbles, distinguishable by color and position.
  • Unclassifiable sound → white “?” bubble, no crash, no special alarm; flash still shows direction.
  • Vibration toggle on → vibration when sounds are captured; toggle off → none (no patterns).

Learner on unknowns: “if it disappears, the situation becomes worse; our goal is to help, so at least there remains a source for the sound.”

Start & Stop
  • Start → toast “Working now”; user may leave the app (or foreground fallback per Open Questions).
  • Pressing the center button again stops the session.

Assumption: the center button is a toggle (no alternative was specified).

States, Empty & Error Cases

FIRST USEGentle permission explanation → system dialog

“I definitely prefer explaining to him gently why we need the permission.”

DENIEDPermission refused

Friendly short message: the app cannot work without the microphone + a route to device settings. Assumption, surfaced for confirmation during review.

QUIETNothing to show

Screen stays calm, no bubbles; session remains active. Assumption: no active-state indicator beyond the start toast was specified.

LEAVING THE APPSession continues

Intended flow; if the platform refuses background capture → fallback: session runs with the app in front (see Open Questions). No data persists between sessions.

Product Decisions

DECIDEDWhy each choice, in her words
  • Icons + flash — chosen over vibration patterns (hard to memorize, cluttered) and text (reading breaks the continuous feel).
  • Unknown sounds shown, not hidden — “if it disappears, the situation becomes worse; our goal is to help.”
  • Session must not obstruct phone use — “a sound-sense must not force you to stare at it,” with a fallback because it needs research.
  • Five categories fixed — “all of them — they are the best choice, most recurring, and most important.”
  • Manual vibration — constant vibration becomes annoying (her own concern).
  • Visual direction — dark, warm/friendly, fairly large clear type.

NOWWhat We're Building

  1. One dark, warm, single-screen app: Start/Stop + vibration toggle.
  2. Gentle mic explanation on first use + friendly denied state.
  3. Session capture classifying into the five fixed categories.
  4. Directional flash (side → fade, strength = sound, color = proximity).
  5. Swipeable icon bubbles · white “?” for unknowns · two-sound display.
  6. Manual vibration during the session.
  7. Start toast, leaving the app without blocking phone use (or documented fallback), working Stop.
  8. Scope's one-minute demo behavior, demonstrable on screen.
DEFERREDDeferred from the POC
  • Always-on 24/7 background with screen off (sleep mode) — needs research; cut to Later in scope.
  • More categories / higher accuracy — five categories prove the kernel.
  • Public deployment — optional; a local recording is fine.
NON-GOALSWhat this will NOT do
  • Recognize every sound in the world (capped at five — “not a super-app”).
  • Vibration patterns per sound type (cut as memorization-heavy).
  • Accounts, history, cloud sync, sharing — nothing implies them.
  • Be an alarm-only danger app — identity is calm, continuous awareness.
